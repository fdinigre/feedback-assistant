# Financial Math mode (v2)

Grade 12 Financial Math: project-based, assessed on the MYP5 mathematics rubric
(criteria A–D, 0–8 each), with formative tests scored as points/total. Course
materials: `~/Desktop/12 TKS Financial Math/Financial Math 2026-27/`
(Foundation, Launch, Banking, Credit, Investing & Retirement, Showcase).

Proposed 2026-09-22 after reading the course; decisions confirmed the same day.

## What the course asks for

- **One summative bundle per unit**, criteria assessed across the whole bundle:
  Banking = client memo (5 sections, 2–3 pp) + live cashflow spreadsheet +
  1-page investigation; Credit = 8–10-slide deck + 60-row auto schedule +
  360-row mortgage schedule or simulation page + trap analysis; Investing =
  9-section report + portfolio one-pager + 5-min client video (script
  600–750 words); Showcase = live defence of the binder (2nd summative C and D).
- **Each rubric doc** carries: level descriptors per criterion, a "where to
  look" table, named failure patterns ("strong A weak C", "strong C weak D",
  "mid B"…), and the feedback rule: **criterion-specific feedback within 5
  school days, 1–2 sentences per criterion naming the current level, what
  reaches the next level, and this student's persona.**
- **Formative spine per unit**: vocab Kahoots (ungraded) · exit ticket every
  period (one number / one equation; Form A/B; key + Secure/Minor/Needs-
  scaffolding triage) · 26-point midpoint quiz (12 MC ×1, 3 calc ×3, 1 short
  answer ×5 with a 0–5 rubric; full key; modified version 8 MC + 1 calc) ·
  Formative B investigation · Formative C writing with a comment-stem
  feedback bank (B-R19) · Summative B first mark, revisable once · peer audit
  · submit · reflection with a 5-skill Secure/OK/Shaky self-check. Credit adds
  two sit-down formative quizzes "recorded as points (formative A)".
- **Personas**: six fictional Saudi clients; each student customises career,
  city and a randomised complication. The persona swap test is the Criterion
  D rule in every rubric.
- **Method choice does not affect the grade**: formula, GDC Finance Solver
  (TI-Nspire) or Sheets all accepted.
- **Spreadsheet checks are mechanical**: live formulas (not hard-coded),
  schedules balancing to ~0, memo numbers traceable to cells; every template
  workbook has Student and Teacher Key tabs.
- Differentiation tiers in every lesson: Stretch / Support / Modified.

## Decisions (confirmed 2026-09-22)

1. **Spreadsheets** are downloaded from Sheets and uploaded as .xlsx files.
2. **Formative tests** are scored by the app (scan → key → grade), with the
   option to upload the teacher's own marks instead (the marks-sheet import).
3. **Modified tier**: no modified students this year, but a per-student switch
   that selects the modified variant of an assessment.
4. **Videos** (Investing): transcribe the script and assess it against C/D.
5. **Money Identity Card**: included in prompts (it carries no names).

## Features

### F1. Class type and scoring
- Class `programme = MYP`, grade 12, type "Financial Math". Assessments are
  either **project deliverables** (criteria A–D, 0–8, no level bands) or
  **formative tests** (points/total, per-question feedback, no level).
- Per-student "modified" switch; an assessment may carry a modified variant
  and alternate forms (A/B) under one gradebook column.

### F2. Formative tests: scan → key → grade
- Blank-test scan → proposed key: MC answers, calculation mark splits, 0–5
  rubric for short answers. Where the Assessment Bank docx has a key, import
  it rather than propose. Teacher approves/edits every item.
- Question types: MC; numeric by any method; linear-equation setup with
  slope/intercept **and their interpretation in words**; short answer with a
  quantitative side and a persona-reasoning side.
- Reuses scan → transcribe → grade → conservative pass → review; the
  marks-sheet import remains available as the alternative.

### F3. Deliverable feedback (memo / investigation / deck / report / script)
- Upload docx/PDF components (+ xlsx, see F4). Criterion-by-criterion
  feedback in the rubric's required form, drawing on the rubric, the "where
  to look" table, the exemplar teacher page, the feedback bank, and the
  student's persona + complication + Money Identity Card. Built on the IA
  draft-feedback / final-marking engine.
- Formative first mark → one revision → summative for Criterion B, both
  versions kept.
- **Client-voice check (C)**: math-voice phrases from the B-R19 translation
  table, unexplained jargon, orphan numbers, missing memo sections, memo
  numbers that disagree with the spreadsheet.
- **Persona test (D)**: client named; complication integrated, not mentioned;
  sentences that would survive a persona swap.
- Unit specifics later: ≤3 lines per slide (Credit); Fisher equation
  "added instead of compounded" (Investing); video script transcribed and
  assessed for C/D.

### F4. Spreadsheet auditor (.xlsx)
- Hard-coded cells where formulas belong; formulas recomputed against the
  Teacher Key tab with the student's inputs; schedules balancing to ~0;
  every number quoted in the memo traced to a cell. This is peer-audit items
  1 and 5 and most of Criterion A's evidence.

### F5. Exit-ticket triage
- Scan cut half-sheets (2 per page, Forms A/B), split, mark against the key,
  and sort students into Secure / Minor / Needs-scaffolding with suggested
  pairings for the next lesson; one-number tickets recorded as a number.

### F6. Binder and deadlines
- Per student: five binder sections, components received/missing,
  formative-before-summative status, revision used, and the 5-school-day
  feedback clock from the Daily Plan dates. Class view of who is late (as the
  IA milestone tracker).

### F7. Client and year context
- Persona (career, city, complication) and Money Identity Card stored per
  student and fed to every prompt.
- Per-criterion trajectory across the four consultations, with the student's
  own skills self-check beside the evidence, for the "how has the student
  grown" comment the Investing rubric asks for.

### F8. Outputs
- Points/total to the gradebook; four criterion levels reported separately
  with the school's MYP-to-grade conversion (never averaged); Toddle-ready
  comments; dossier as today.

## Non-functional

- Same privacy rule as v1: student names never leave the machine; personas
  are fictional and may be sent; the Money Identity Card is sent
  pseudonymised.
- Everything the AI proposes is reviewable and editable before anything is
  exported.
